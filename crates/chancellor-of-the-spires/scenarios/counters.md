# Counters

### 1. Proliferating a player adds one of every kind of counter they have

**Cards:** [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician]
**Board:** P1 controls Yawgmoth, Thran Physician, has five energy counters
and five poison counters, {B}{B} available and a card in hand.
**Does:** P1 activates Yawgmoth's proliferate ability, discarding a card,
and chooses P1.
**Then:** P1 has six energy and six poison counters. P1 couldn't have
added energy alone: choosing a player gives one more of each kind.
**Because:** CR 701.34a: proliferate chooses permanents and players, not
counters, then gives each one more of each kind already there.
CR 115.10a: the choice isn't targeting.
**Source:** [TR&CI 228: How Does Proliferate Actually Work - Can You Respond & Can You Choose Which Counters](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BDfJwNLtwQs)
**Engine:** counters on players (energy, poison) and proliferate; an
activated ability costing a discard runs (Trade Routes).

### 2. A ward cost a player pays by getting counters is the payer's counters, which Vorinclex halves

**Cards:** [The Serpent Society], [Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider], [Lightning Bolt]
**Board:** P1 controls The Serpent Society and Vorinclex, Monstrous
Raider. P2 has no poison counters.
**Does:** P2 casts Lightning Bolt targeting The Serpent Society. Ward
triggers; P1 controls it. As it resolves, P2 chooses to pay.
**Then:** P2 gets two poison counters (five halved, rounded down), and
Lightning Bolt isn't countered.
**Because:** CR 702.21a: ward counters the spell unless "that player"
pays; CR 118.1, CR 118.12a: the player paying a cost carries it out, so
P2 is the one putting the counters on, and Vorinclex's opponent clause
applies (Vorinclex's ruling: who puts the counters on decides).
**Unsure:** the CR says who pays a cost but not who "puts" counters a
player "gets" as a cost; the video's answer (two) follows CR 118.1, and no
ruling on The Serpent Society says so.
**Source:** [TR&CI 223: The Serpent Society & Ward - Get Five Poison Counters (Complications w/ Vorinclex)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PsMdjUytjcM)
**Engine:** ward (carried unread today), an "unless" paid in counters, and
Vorinclex's two counter replacements.

### 3. A player who can't get counters can't pay "get five poison counters", so the spell is countered

**Cards:** [The Serpent Society], [Solemnity], [Lightning Bolt]
**Board:** P1 controls The Serpent Society. Solemnity is on the
battlefield. P2 has no poison counters.
**Does:** P2 casts Lightning Bolt targeting The Serpent Society. Ward
triggers and resolves.
**Then:** P2 can't pay; Lightning Bolt is countered, and P2 has no poison
counters.
**Because:** CR 614.17b: if an event can't happen, a player can't choose
to pay a cost that includes it. CR 118.3: a cost can't be paid without
the means to pay it fully. CR 702.21a.
**Source:** [TR&CI 223: The Serpent Society & Ward - Get Five Poison Counters (Complications w/ Vorinclex)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PsMdjUytjcM)
**Engine:** ward, poison counters, and Solemnity's "Players can't get
counters". "Counter target spell unless its controller pays" runs (Mana
Leak); this is the same "unless" with an unpayable cost.
